## Lab Overview
In this lab, our group explored how model complexity affects machine learning performance. We used a synthetic sine-wave dataset and Polynomial Regression to compare models with degrees 1, 4, and 15. We also created learning curves to understand underfitting, overfitting, and the bias-variance tradeoff by comparing training and cross-validation scores.

## Results
- **Degree 1:** Underfitting because the model was too simple to capture the curved pattern.
- **Degree 4:** Provided a better fit by following the general pattern without focusing too much on noise.
- **Degree 15:** Showed signs of overfitting, with high training scores but much lower cross-validation scores.
